How to Play

1. Move & Look

WASD walk · Drag to look · F flashlight · SPACE climb hill · E use / grab / drop

2. Investigate

Click or tap objects to examine. Grab and move clues. Find enough clues before you can leave a level.

3. Use Every Tool

Click food, water, or medical supplies to hold them, then press E to use. Find the wall switches; use the map and level music controls.

4. Ask Anything

Search for clues, question characters, climb the manor stairs, and connect the evidence to move the story forward.

"Don't choose what the game lets you do.
Tell the game what you want to do."
